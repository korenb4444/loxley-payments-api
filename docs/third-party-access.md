# Third-party access to this repository

Codebridge Labs engineers hold read access to `loxley-payments-api` through the
`codebridge-contractors` team, for the merchant onboarding workstream.

- Access is reviewed every quarter by the Platform Engineering lead.
- Contractor accounts are not granted production database access from this repository;
  requests go through the Production Access Runbook.
- Offboarding is triggered by the vendor's exit checklist in the Vendor Register.

Last review: 8 September 2026.
